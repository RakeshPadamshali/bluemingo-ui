Pulpit — BRM · Roughing (acknowledge mode)

One screen per control pulpit: the entries acknowledged at the previous pulpit appear here automatically; select and acknowledge (no re-entry of batch data), declare the product (prime / hot-out / indirect hot-out / cobble / alternate route / scrap), see the SOP parameters, the notes and the downtime timer; the acknowledgement activates the entry at the next pulpit.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: Acknowledge B031138 at Roughing → it appears at Finishing, blocked there until the pop-up instruction is acknowledged · declare B031139 COBBLE (downtime starts by itself — stop it with a reason) · HOT_OUT / INDIRECT_HOT_OUT (→ Gas cutting) · ALT_ROUTE to the cooling bed (infeasible — shift supervisor) · Caster CCM-1: QA feedback pop-up needs a remark, never blocks, escalates at 10:44 · Furnace → Roughing hand-over · Downtime: start / stop · Wallboard view.
