Mill Reports

The reports of the Mill Operations epic over the views of this design (daily production, SO-wise, heat yield, process parameters, monthly trends, delay / cobble) plus the PPC plan-vs-actual and WIP views; the ERP confirmation status waits for the PPC outbox (SAP: deferred); configurable layouts are the deferred document service.
Prototype on sample data — a read screen; a reload resets.
