Hot-Out & Re-roll

Direct hot-out (charged, not rolled → back to charging), indirect hot-out (partially rolled → cut 2–6 pieces, re-measure, classify rollable / non-rollable, re-declare, re-sequence), overstay (Quality decision before re-roll); re-weigh on re-charge, loss booked once, excluded from the bundle weight.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: HO-2026-0187 — act as Quality and decide piece B031140-02 REWORK to the grinding line (code kept), Scrap piece 3, then Re-sequence rollable pieces (the event closes) · HO-2026-0186 overstay — Re-charge is refused until Quality decides RE_ROLL_OK, then re-weigh books the loss once · HO-2026-0185 — a second re-weigh is refused · Declare hot-out: B031139 indirect → cut into pieces, edit lengths / weights (loss recomputes), classify, save; B031143 or a wrong type is refused.
