WIP & stock visibility

Shortage watch for the released blocks, stage-wise and equipment-wise WIP, incoming bought and transferred material, restricted vs unrestricted stock, the free FG stock with its ageing, per-order pegged and eligible free stock, and the billets of an order at charging with the charges seen. Code state is derived by PPC; locations are read from the Quality module.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets; this screen writes nothing. Try: click a counter (it filters the WIP) · change "as at" in the shortage watch · Cover on a short block · filter or group the WIP and click a row for its lots and code state · click "free FG" for the free FG stock — filter by ageing, see why each lot is free and the open lines it fits, and Allocate one in Swapping · pick an order for its free stock (Open swap) and its billets at charging.

Shortage watch — released blocks, next 36 has at cover = pegged cleared + under inspection + casting arrivals before the start · alert 12 h ahead (policy PPC_SHORTAGE_LEAD_HOURS) · tolerance 2 %

WIP by stage and equipment code state derived by PPC — gross: SMS lot not yet cleared · net: cleared by Quality · G&D: last operation grinding · location from the Quality module · restricted = Quality state or a lot hold

Free FG stock FG lots with no customer order — no order link, or on an internal NCO or make-to-stock order · ageing from the day the lot became free · Allocate a lot to an open line it fits in Swapping (stage FG); Dispatch then posts it in SAP

Incoming — purchase orders and STO expected from the SAP PO / STO message · received and inspected through the Quality raw-material receipt · counts in netting on acceptance
