Order Hold & Release

PPC Super User screen: single or mass hold / release with a reason from the hold-reason master. An order hold holds the order's lots through the existing lot hold — a held lot leaves every queue and cannot be charged or confirmed — and blocks the order's plan entries in the Allocation application; held lines also vanish from every PPC scheduling screen.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: switch to Planner (hold is refused) · tick lines → Mass hold / Mass release · Hold SO-88140/20 with "Part — chosen lots" (B031099 is skipped: it has another hold) · Release part of SO-91802/10 — B031090, B031091 and 100 t not yet made (the rest stays held) · release it whole (B031096 stays held by Quality) · in its lots, let Quality free B031096 or the planned heat be cast · SO-91950/10 is a Marketing hold from SFDC — let SFDC release it · filter by source · hold a schedule.
Single and mass hold / release need ORDER_HOLD, ORDER_RELEASE, ORDER_MASS_HOLD and ORDER_MASS_RELEASE, seeded to the PPC Super User only (a plant may grant ORDER_HOLD to more roles in the Privileged Actions master). A hold covers the order, one line — whole, or only its unreleased balance — chosen lots, or a schedule; a release frees the whole hold or part of it — chosen lots and / or a quantity not yet made. A hold placed in SAP or SFDC (for example by Marketing) arrives with who placed it and applies automatically — through the order upload until those interfaces exist — and is released automatically when the source releases it. Every event is logged with user, time and reason.

Schedule holds scope SCHEDULE · PPC holds the schedule's lots through the lot hold, so they leave the queue and cannot be charged or confirmed · reason mandatory
