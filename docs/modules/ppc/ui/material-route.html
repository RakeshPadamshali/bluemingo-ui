Material Route

The composite route: the process stages and the quality work each one owes, resolved from the PSN and the order attributes at release and pinned to the order line; batches inherit it. Conditional stages show their trigger, skipped stages show why, and every deviation from the standard route is listed. PPC keeps each lot's route, flags a lot the pulpit sends elsewhere and may correct it through the existing next-operation assignment; otherwise the route changes only through the pulpit, a disposition, a usage decision or a path change.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: open B031143 or heat 2627-01120 · switch the order line (double rolling; a blocked release; a stock route) · Explain the routing · click a stage for where its quality content came from · Refresh (the lab result arrives) · in Lot route, Correct B031143 or Accept B031144 (job work stage inserted).
