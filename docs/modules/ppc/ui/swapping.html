PPC decides the swap — six scenarios plus reverse, manual or auto, validated by the swap rules and logged; the Allocation application executes each line with its existing steps and, for lots already produced, the swap bridge moves the lot's order. Swaps happen before release — at sequencing or in WIP; none at the furnace. Free FG is allocated to an order at the stage FG and posted in SAP; FG moved between orders stays in SAP.
