Downstream Schedules — Annealing · GMM · Bright Bar

Downstream schedules by day, week, month or campaign, with supply condition as a campaign basis: annealing charges by type and load group within furnace load and cycle; GMM lots of a configurable number of heats under one sales order by default, split into customer batches at FG; Bright Bar by output size. Each line is released with or without WIP; a unit may change size, grade, customer priority or sequence inside a released schedule — the system checks it, then PPC-HOD approves.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: Annealing — Edit A-03 (untick the 17-4PH lot) → Release; Build charge (mixing AUS-A with FER groups or over the furnace max is refused); click a release mode · GMM — Add heat / Close lot / Split customer batches · Bright Bar — Release · Changes — act as Unit in-charge → Propose (each preset shows its checks) → act as PPC-HOD → Approve.
