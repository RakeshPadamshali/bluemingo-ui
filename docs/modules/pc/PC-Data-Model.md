# Process Control: data-model design (JSW Salem Works, Phase 2 pass 5)

**Date:** 2026-09-15 · **Scope:** backlog area 4 (14 items) — BRD §5 Process Control (5.1 parameter master and validation, 5.1.1 deviation alert rules and escalation matrix, 5.2 reporting and external sharing, 5.3 acceptance criteria with five additional points); workbook *Process Control* (30 rows); stories F2.1-01…06, F2.2-01…04, F2.3-01…05; requirements PC-001…008, PDA-001…005. · **Method:** extracted from the production-confirmation code and the live database (`bluemingo_mes_ambica`) on 2026-09-15; nothing in either product is changed from here. · **Companions:** `Requests-PC.md` (change requests), the Master Data design (`../mdm/MDM-Data-Model.md` §3.7 — the parameter master extension), the Operations design (`../ops/OPS-Data-Model.md` §2, §4, §11 — pulpits, SOP panel, Level-2 messages), the Customer Quality design (`../cq/CQ-PSN-Data-Model.md` — PSN lifecycle and unit view) and the QA Data-Model §31 (Quality touchpoints).

---

## 0. Organisation

### 0.1 Ownership and landing
Process Control is a **platform** module: the parameter master, the captured values, the deviation events, the alert ledger, the correction loop, the length profile, the trials and the publication ledger live in the production-confirmation application (`mes_*`) and are raised as change requests **PC-R-01 … PC-R-11** in `Requests-PC.md`. Five touchpoints are Quality-owned and land as QA Data-Model **§31**: the parameter panel on inspection and testing, the deviation disposition and the usage-decision interlock (with the NCR link), the notification-rule master extension (severity, conditions, escalation, multiple recipients), the suspect-flag verification in offline inspection, and the PSN block on repeat rejection. Nothing inside either application is coded from here.

### 0.2 Conventions
As the QA Data-Model §2 and the MDM / PPC / Operations designs §0.2: PK `<entity>_id bigint`; **`+ audit tail`**; `(Y)` nullable; scope key set S and resolution rule **R-S**; `effective_from/to` informational until P3. Product-agnostic per D0: units and machines are `mes_operations` / `mes_equipments` rows; parameters are `mes_global_attributes` rows; severities, breach classes, capture statuses and dispositions are data. Flags: `[P2]` the notification engine is deferred — rules and the alert ledger are designed, delivery is not; `[P5]` the OPC-UA / MQTT transport is deferred — the critical matrix and the publication ledger are designed; `[D-08]` Level-2 capture is designed on the Operations message ledger under the assumption that it is in scope; `[D-14]` heat-wise trials designed in scope.

### 0.3 Live facts that shaped the design (verified 2026-09-15)
- **Master:** `mes_process_attributes` has 6 rows, all under one process (PR_PLANNING) — heat treatment: heating rate (target 120, no band), loading temperature 650–700, soaking time (target 120), soaking temperature 1,050–1,070; packing: bundle weight 1,800–2,200; billet cutting: Demand UID (DYNAMIC). Every row says `default_captured_from = PLC` although no automation feed exists. Key = process + operation + attribute — no equipment, no PSN, no grouping (the MDM design §3.7 adds equipment, PSN, grade family, size family, shape / size band, `validation_mode`, `is_critical`, `display_sequence`, `corrected_value_capture`, `priority`, effective dating; the Operations design §4 adds `parameter_group`, `zone_code`, `l2_tag`, dwell limits).
- **Attribute dictionary:** `mes_global_attributes` 191 rows — RANGE 69, VALUE 63, DYNAMIC 59, with `attribute_value_min / max` and a unit; **`mes_attribute_possible_value`** (attribute → allowed value) exists — the LIST validation has a home. `mes_operation_attributes` carries `use_for_pc_entry`, `use_as_input / output`, `display_seq` per operation.
- **Capture:** `mes_process_parameters_captured` (confirmation, attribute, `attribute_value varchar`, `captured_from`) — **0 rows** live; the confirmation card shows target, band and unit, sanitises numeric input for RANGE attributes, computes the IN RANGE / NEAR / OUT badge **in the browser with a hard-coded 5 % grace band**, never persists the outcome, never blocks Confirm & Submit, drops blank readings from the payload, and accepts VALUE-type attributes with no rule (status ENTERED). `captured_from` is a label chosen by the operator (PLC / Manual / Operator).
- **Reversal:** `mes_reversed_process_parameters_captured` mirrors the readings on reversal (rules R7 / R8) — traceability across reversal and rework is built.
- **Read-back:** readings are queried by confirmation id only (the PC detail drawer, the Genealogy drill-down) — no search by heat or batch, no report among the 19, no dashboard.
- **Not process control:** `mes_operations.deviation_mode = AUTO_DEALLOCATE` and `mes_production_allocation_deviation` concern planned-material quantity deviations (`SmaDeviationHandler`); `mes_pc_screen_config.use_compliance_metric` (2 of 6 rows) is the yield-compliance metric; `mes_tolerance` is empty; `mes_sample_dimension_attribute` maps sample dimensions to attributes.
- **Quality side:** the QA design's Usage-Decision screen carries a read-only casting-parameter validation panel over `mes_process_parameters_captured` (interface IF-03); `mes_qc_ncr.nc_against` already admits PROCESS; `mes_qc_notification_rule` (designed) has one recipient role, channel, frequency, lead time — no severity, condition, acknowledgement or escalation; the CQ design makes the event vocabulary data (`mes_qc_notification_event`), gives the PSN a lifecycle with BLOCKED / STOPPED and a unit-wise view (`v_qc_psn_unit_view`); `mes_qc_screen_policy` scopes policies by screen × operation / form / category / customer / grade.
- **Absent in both code bases:** deviation entity, severity matrix, alert acknowledgement / escalation, interlock on clearance, corrected value, mandatory capture, LIST / BOOLEAN validation, setup sheet, online NC, suspect flag, length-wise profile, trial object, OPC-UA / MQTT, Level-2 / PLC client.

## 1. Cross-cutting decisions for Process Control

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **M-PC-01** | **One parameter master, completed — not a second one.** `mes_process_attributes` (extended by MDM §3.7 and OPS §4) gains the last missing facts: value type and accepted values, mandatory and skip rules, a configurable grace band, the capture stage, the display flags for lab and inspection, the trend window and the sharing topic. Resolution stays R-S with the explicit precedence PSN → grade family → size family → equipment → operation default. | PC-001, PC-004, rows 1, 2, 7, 11; the workbook's own recommendation. |
| **M-PC-02** | **The captured row is the record of truth for one reading:** original value immutable, corrected value beside it with reason and user, limits snapshotted at capture time, the source (manual / Level 2 / fallback / override), the breach outcome and the deviation it raised. Nothing is computed only in the browser. | Rows 5, 6, 9, 10, 22; PC-002/003; 5.3 additional point 4. |
| **M-PC-03** | **A deviation is an event with a severity, a disposition and a hold.** `mes_process_deviation` is written by the capture (and by the Level-2 message) whenever a reading breaches its rule; the severity comes from a configurable matrix; the disposition (accept with reason, rework, reject, override) is recorded by the roles the matrix names; CRITICAL and MAJOR deviations hold the material through the existing inventory hold until dispositioned. | PDA-001…005, rows 14, 19, 21. |
| **M-PC-04** | **Alerts are a ledger, delivery is the deferred engine** `[P2]`. `mes_process_alert` records who was to be told, by which channel, when it was acknowledged and when it escalated; the rules (multi-role, shift-aware, severity-driven, escalate-after) are the Quality notification-rule master extended in QA §31. | Rows 17, 18, 20; PDA-001…003. |
| **M-PC-05** | **Level-2 capture rides the Operations message ledger** `[D-08]`: PARAMETER_ACTUAL messages write captured rows with source L2; a parameter expected from automation and not received within its window is a FALLBACK capture flagged for Quality; auto-captured values lock against overtyping unless an override reason is recorded. | Rows 8, 9; F2.2-02/03. |
| **M-PC-06** | **The rolling correction loop is five linked records:** setup sheet (what the mill was set to) → sample (existing) → online non-conformance → parameter correction → suspect flag on the batch cleared by offline inspection. Each step references the previous one, so the loop is reportable end to end. | 5.3 additional point 3, row 23. |
| **M-PC-07** | **Length-wise quality is a profile object with an apportionment,** not more scalar readings: segments per input billet from the gauge, statistics per output bundle or coil derived at the split. Interface deferred. | 5.3 additional point 1, row 24. |
| **M-PC-08** | **Trials are an approved envelope per heat** `[D-14]`: while a trial is running, readings are judged against the trial envelope and flagged TRIAL instead of raising standard deviations; the approval is a named workflow (D-03) and the follow-up report reads the flagged captures. | 5.3 additional point 5, row 26, OPEN-GEN-1. |
| **M-PC-09** | **External sharing is a matrix plus a ledger** `[P5]`: `is_critical` and the topic template on the master define the critical-parameter matrix at batch-id level; `mes_parameter_publication` records every value published (or queued) per node / topic; the OPC-UA server and MQTT broker are the deferred transport. | PC-008, rows 15, 16. |
| **M-PC-10** | **Masters, screens and lifecycles come from the other designs:** the parameter master extension (MDM), the pulpit screens, SOP panel, wallboard and message ledger (Operations), the PSN unit view, documents and lifecycle (Customer Quality), the NCR / CAPA, notification rules, sampling and clearance (Quality module). No Process Control copy. | One book of record. |

## 2. Parameter master — completion *(rows 1, 2, 7, 11, 29, 30; PC-001, PC-004, F2.1-01, F2.1-06)* — PLATFORM (master owned by Process Control; extension of MDM §3.7 and OPS §4)

- `mes_process_attributes` gains **`value_type varchar(10)`** (NUMERIC / LIST / BOOLEAN / TEXT — derived from the attribute type when blank: RANGE → NUMERIC, VALUE → LIST when possible values exist else TEXT) · `accepted_values_json jsonb` (Y — the acceptable subset of the attribute's possible values for LIST) · `expected_boolean boolean` (Y — the acceptable answer for BOOLEAN) · **`is_mandatory boolean`** · `skip_allowed boolean` (a mandatory parameter may be skipped only with a documented reason when true) · **`grace_pct numeric(7,3)`** (Y — the NEAR band as a percentage of the tolerance band; blank = no grace; replaces the hard-coded 5 %) · `capture_stage varchar(15)` (OPERATION / TESTING / INSPECTION / ALL — where the parameter is captured) · `display_on_lab boolean` · `display_on_inspection boolean` · `trend_window_n int` (Y — readings shown on the trend) · `sop_document_ref varchar(255)` (Y — the SOP / work instruction shown beside the parameter) · `share_topic varchar(255)` (Y — OPC-UA node or MQTT topic template, e.g. `jsw/{unit}/{batch}/{parameter}`; used when `is_critical`) · `default_severity varchar(10)` (Y — MINOR / MAJOR / CRITICAL when no severity rule matches).
- **R-PC-01 — resolution precedence (PC-004, F2.1-06):** the set shown at a station resolves by R-S over (operation, equipment, PSN, grade family, size family, shape / size band) with seeded priorities PSN 10 → grade family 20 → size family 30 → equipment 40 → operation default 90; the most specific active row per attribute wins; the resolved row's limits are snapshotted on every capture (§3) so a later master change never rewrites history.
- **R-PC-02 — value types (row 11):** NUMERIC validates against lower / upper with the grace band; LIST validates against `accepted_values_json` (a value outside the attribute's possible values is refused at entry, a possible-but-not-accepted value is a breach); BOOLEAN validates against `expected_boolean`; TEXT is recorded only. A breach of any type raises a deviation (§4).
- **R-PC-03 — coverage (row 29):** `v_pc_master_coverage` lists every operation with the Process Parameters card enabled and the count of master rows, mandatory rows and critical rows, so the unit-by-unit definition exercise against the log books is tracked.
- **`v_pc_master_coverage`** — operation × equipment: card enabled, master rows, mandatory, critical, Level-2 expected, last capture date.

## 3. Capture — the reading as the record of truth *(rows 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 22, 28; PC-002, PC-003, PC-005, F2.1-02/03, F2.2-01…04, 5.3 additional point 4)* — PLATFORM

- `mes_process_parameters_captured` gains **`original_value varchar(100)`** (Y — the first value entered or received; immutable) · **`corrected_value varchar(100)`** (Y) · `corrected_by bigint` (Y) · `corrected_at timestamptz` (Y) · `correction_reason varchar(255)` (Y) · `correction_id` FK `mes_parameter_correction` (Y — when the correction is part of the rolling loop) · **`capture_status varchar(12)`** (CAPTURED / AUTO / FALLBACK / OVERRIDDEN / SKIPPED / MISSING) · `skip_reason varchar(255)` (Y) · `captured_at timestamptz` · `captured_by bigint` (Y) · `equipment_id` FK `mes_equipments` (Y) · `batch_id` FK `mes_batches` (Y) · `heat_number varchar(100)` (Y) · `schedule_material_child_id` FK `mes_schedule_material_childs` (Y) · `tdc_id` FK `mes_tdc_input` (Y — the PSN resolved for the material) · `process_attribute_id` FK `mes_process_attributes` (Y — the resolved master row) · `target_snapshot numeric(18,4)` (Y) · `lower_snapshot numeric(18,4)` (Y) · `upper_snapshot numeric(18,4)` (Y) · `grace_pct_snapshot numeric(7,3)` (Y) · **`breach_class varchar(15)`** (Y — IN_RANGE / NEAR / BELOW_MIN / ABOVE_MAX / VALUE_NOT_ALLOWED / MISSING / TRIAL) · `deviation_id` FK `mes_process_deviation` (Y) · `l2_message_id` FK `mes_l2_message` (Y — the PARAMETER_ACTUAL message that wrote the row) · `expected_source varchar(10)` (Y — MANUAL / L2 from the master) · `trial_id` FK `mes_process_trial` (Y) · `is_mandatory_snapshot boolean` (Y).
- `mes_pc_screen_config` gains **`enforce_mandatory_parameters boolean`** · `deviation_hold_severity varchar(10)` (Y — the lowest severity that raises the hold: MAJOR by default) · `show_spec_panel boolean` (customer specification and SOP references on the unit screen) · `l2_capture_window_s int` (Y — seconds to wait for the automation value before FALLBACK) · `lock_auto_values boolean` (auto-captured values cannot be overtyped without an override reason).
- **R-PC-04 — mandatory capture (row 7):** with `enforce_mandatory_parameters` the confirmation is refused while a mandatory parameter of the resolved set has no value; a mandatory parameter with `skip_allowed` may be submitted with a `skip_reason` (status SKIPPED, breach MISSING, a MINOR deviation); every parameter of the set gets a row — blank optional ones as MISSING — so completeness is reportable.
- **R-PC-05 — validation at entry (PC-003, row 5):** NUMERIC: within [lower, upper] → IN_RANGE; outside by at most `grace_pct` of the band → NEAR (a deviation of severity MINOR unless the matrix says otherwise); beyond → BELOW_MIN / ABOVE_MAX; LIST / BOOLEAN per R-PC-02; the outcome is persisted as `breach_class` and, when not IN_RANGE, a deviation row is written (§4) — the validation mode of the master row (WARN / BLOCK / DEVIATION) decides whether the confirmation continues, is refused or continues with the deviation open. Worked example: soaking temperature 1,050–1,070 °C, grace 5 % of the 20 °C band = 1 °C; reading 1,070.8 → NEAR (MINOR); reading 1,078 → ABOVE_MAX (severity from the matrix: 8 °C = 40 % of the band → MAJOR).
- **R-PC-06 — standard, actual, corrected (5.3 additional point 4, row 22):** the card shows three columns; the original value is never overwritten; a correction writes `corrected_value` with reason, user and time; validation and the deviation refer to the corrected value when present (the deviation keeps both); the report shows all three.
- **R-PC-07 — Level-2 capture and failover (F2.2-02/03, rows 8, 9) `[D-08]`:** a parameter whose resolved row has `default_captured_from = L2` (its `l2_tag` on the row) is filled by the PARAMETER_ACTUAL message of the Operations ledger with `capture_status = AUTO` and `captured_from = L2` — the label is set only when the value actually arrived; no message within `l2_capture_window_s` marks the row FALLBACK and lets the operator type the value; overtyping an AUTO value needs an override reason (`capture_status = OVERRIDDEN`, original kept); `v_pc_fallback_exceptions` lists FALLBACK and OVERRIDDEN rows for Quality.
- **R-PC-08 — traceability (PC-002, row 10):** every row carries batch, heat, piece and PSN; `v_pc_parameter_trace` is searchable by heat number, batch and unit and feeds the Genealogy drill-down; reversal keeps the mirror rows (built) and the report flags them as reversed.
- **`v_pc_parameter_trace`** — unit × equipment × heat × batch × parameter: target, limits and grace at capture, original, corrected, source, status, breach, deviation, trial, confirmation, reversed flag.
- **`v_pc_fallback_exceptions`** — FALLBACK and OVERRIDDEN captures with the expected source, the message status and the override reason.

- **`mes_process_deviation`** — `deviation_id` PK · `deviation_no varchar(30)` UQ · `captured_id` FK `mes_process_parameters_captured` · `process_attribute_id` FK `mes_process_attributes` (Y) · `attribute_id` FK `mes_global_attributes` · `operation_id` FK `mes_operations` · `equipment_id` FK `mes_equipments` (Y) · `pulpit_code varchar(30)` (Y) · `confirmation_id` FK `mes_production_confirmation` (Y) · `batch_id` FK `mes_batches` (Y) · `heat_number varchar(100)` (Y) · `schedule_material_child_id` FK `mes_schedule_material_childs` (Y) · `tdc_id` FK `mes_tdc_input` (Y) · `order_line_id` FK `mes_order_line_items` (Y) · `breach_class varchar(15)` (NEAR / BELOW_MIN / ABOVE_MAX / VALUE_NOT_ALLOWED / MISSING) · `actual_value varchar(100)` (Y) · `limit_value numeric(18,4)` (Y — the limit breached) · `magnitude_pct numeric(9,3)` (Y — breach as a percentage of the band) · **`severity varchar(10)`** (MINOR / MAJOR / CRITICAL) · `severity_rule_id` FK `mes_deviation_severity_rule` (Y) · `is_critical_parameter boolean` · `trial_id` FK `mes_process_trial` (Y — judged against a trial envelope) · `source varchar(10)` (MANUAL / L2 / SYSTEM) · `raised_at timestamptz` · `shift_id` FK `mes_shifts` (Y) · `hold_id` FK `mes_inventory_holds` (Y — the interlock hold) · **`status varchar(15)`** (OPEN / ACKNOWLEDGED / DISPOSITIONED / OVERRIDDEN / CLOSED / VOID) · `acknowledged_by bigint` (Y) · `acknowledged_at timestamptz` (Y) · `disposition varchar(15)` (Y — ACCEPT / REWORK / REJECT / OVERRIDE) · `disposition_reason varchar(255)` (Y) · `dispositioned_by bigint` (Y) · `dispositioned_role varchar(50)` (Y) · `dispositioned_at timestamptz` (Y) · `ncr_ref varchar(100)` (Y — the Quality NCR number when one is raised) · `online_nc_id` FK `mes_online_nc` (Y) · `remarks varchar(255)` (Y) · **+ audit tail**.
- **`mes_deviation_severity_rule`** — `rule_id` PK · `rule_code varchar(50)` UQ · S (`factory_id`, `operation_id`, `equipment_id`, `product_category_id`, `shape`, `size_min/max`, `grade`, `grade_series`, `customer_id`, `tdc_id`) · `attribute_id` FK `mes_global_attributes` (Y — blank = every parameter) · `breach_class varchar(15)` (Y — blank = any) · `magnitude_from_pct numeric(9,3)` (Y) · `magnitude_to_pct numeric(9,3)` (Y) · `critical_parameter_only boolean` · **`severity varchar(10)`** (MINOR / MAJOR / CRITICAL) · `channel varchar(20)` (POPUP / POPUP_EMAIL / POPUP_EMAIL_ESCALATE) · `notify_roles_json jsonb` (Y — e.g. SHIFT_OPERATOR, SHIFT_SUPERVISOR, QUALITY_ON_DUTY) · `escalate_after_min int` (Y) · `escalation_role varchar(50)` (Y — e.g. UNIT_QUALITY_HEAD) · `raise_hold boolean` · `raise_ncr boolean` · `disposition_roles_json jsonb` (Y — roles allowed to disposition) · `priority int` · `effective_from/to` · **+ audit tail**.
- **R-PC-09 — severity resolution (PDA-002):** the matching rules by R-S plus attribute, breach class and magnitude band; the lowest priority wins; no match → the master row's `default_severity`, else MINOR; a critical parameter never resolves below MAJOR. Seeds: NEAR → MINOR (pop-up); breach up to 50 % of the band → MAJOR (pop-up + email, escalate 15 min to Shift Supervisor); beyond 50 % or a critical parameter → CRITICAL (pop-up + email + escalation to Unit Quality Head, hold, NCR).
- **R-PC-10 — one event per reading (PDA-005):** a captured row raises at most one deviation; a correction that brings the reading in range closes it with disposition ACCEPT and the correction reference; a reversal voids it; the deviation number is `DEV-{unit}-{yyyymm}-{seq}`.
- **`v_pc_deviation_log`** — every deviation with parameter, material, severity, channel used, alert raised / acknowledged / escalated times, disposition, role and reason, NCR reference (the Process Deviation Alert Log of PDA-005).

## 5. Alerts, acknowledgement and escalation *(rows 17, 18, 20; PDA-001, PDA-003)* `[P2 — engine deferred, ledger designed]` — PLATFORM (rules in the Quality notification-rule master, QA §31.3)

- **`mes_process_alert`** — `alert_id` PK · `deviation_id` FK `mes_process_deviation` · `rule_ref varchar(50)` (Y — the notification rule code) · `severity varchar(10)` · `channel varchar(20)` · `recipient_role varchar(50)` · `recipient_user_id` FK `mes_user` (Y — resolved from the shift roster when shift-aware) · `shift_id` FK `mes_shifts` (Y) · `pulpit_code varchar(30)` (Y — location-scoped alerts for the wallboard) · `raised_at timestamptz` · `delivered_at timestamptz` (Y) · **`status varchar(15)`** (RAISED / DELIVERED / ACKNOWLEDGED / ESCALATED / EXPIRED / CLOSED) · `acknowledged_by bigint` (Y) · `acknowledged_at timestamptz` (Y) · `escalation_level int` (0 = first recipient) · `escalated_from_alert_id` FK `mes_process_alert` (Y) · `escalate_at timestamptz` (Y — raised + escalate-after) · `message_text varchar(1000)` (Y) · **+ audit tail**.
- **R-PC-11 — parallel routing (PDA-001, row 18):** one deviation produces one alert row per recipient role of the resolved rule (operating shift and the Quality on-duty contact in parallel); shift-aware roles resolve to the users rostered on the shift of `raised_at`; the wallboard of the pulpit shows the open alerts of its location (Operations design §2).
- **R-PC-12 — acknowledgement and escalation (PDA-003, row 20):** CRITICAL and MAJOR alerts not acknowledged by `escalate_at` create an ESCALATED row for the escalation role (level + 1) and keep escalating along the hierarchy (Shift Supervisor → Unit Quality Head) until acknowledged; acknowledgement is recorded with user and time on the alert and on the deviation; MINOR alerts expire at shift end.

## 6. Interlock and disposition *(row 21; PDA-004)* — PLATFORM (disposition by Quality, QA §31.2)

- **R-PC-13 — hold on deviation (PDA-004):** a deviation whose severity is at or above `deviation_hold_severity` raises an inventory hold (the built `mes_inventory_holds`) with the seeded hold reason PROCESS_DEVIATION on the batch or piece and links it (`hold_id`); the material is not schedulable, chargeable or auto-clearable while the hold is open (the built HeatChemistryGate / hold checks, the Quality auto-clearance rule).
- **R-PC-14 — disposition (PDA-004):** the deviation is released by a disposition — ACCEPT with a reason, REWORK (the built rework flow), REJECT (the Quality salvage / NCR flow) — by a role listed in the rule's `disposition_roles_json`, or OVERRIDE by the privileged action DEVIATION_OVERRIDE; the hold is released with the disposition; CRITICAL deviations (or `raise_ncr`) open a Quality NCR with `nc_against = PROCESS` and the NCR number is written back (`ncr_ref`).
- **R-PC-15 — clearance interlock (PDA-004):** the Quality usage-decision auto-clearance refuses the batch while an OPEN or ACKNOWLEDGED deviation of severity at or above the hold severity exists (QA §31.2); the manual usage decision shows the open deviations and needs the disposition first.

## Annex C — Seeds
- Value types: NUMERIC · LIST · BOOLEAN · TEXT. Capture status: CAPTURED · AUTO · FALLBACK · OVERRIDDEN · SKIPPED · MISSING. Breach classes: IN_RANGE · NEAR · BELOW_MIN · ABOVE_MAX · VALUE_NOT_ALLOWED · MISSING · TRIAL.
- Severity matrix: NEAR → MINOR (POPUP); breach ≤ 50 % of the band → MAJOR (POPUP_EMAIL, escalate 15 min → SHIFT_SUPERVISOR, hold); breach > 50 % of the band or critical parameter → CRITICAL (POPUP_EMAIL_ESCALATE → UNIT_QUALITY_HEAD, hold, NCR). Hold severity default MAJOR. Grace band default 5 % of the band where the unit asks for it.
- Resolution priorities: PSN 10 · grade family 20 · size family 30 · equipment 40 · operation default 90.
- Roles: SHIFT_OPERATOR · SHIFT_SUPERVISOR · QUALITY_ON_DUTY · UNIT_QUALITY_HEAD · PROCESS_CONTROL_HEAD. Privileged actions: DEVIATION_OVERRIDE · TRIAL_APPROVE · AUTO_VALUE_OVERRIDE · SETUP_SHEET_ISSUE.
- Hold reason: PROCESS_DEVIATION (Process deviation under disposition). Level-2 capture window 30 s; MINOR alerts expire at shift end.
- Length profile: segment 6 m; quality index = 1 − (reject segments + 0.5 × watch segments) ÷ segments. PSN block threshold 15 t per heat and defect.
