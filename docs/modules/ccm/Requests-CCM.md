## MES PC items

### CCM-R-01 — Order-arrival match against active complaint watches; the COMPLAINT WATCH chip on the Order Book and the scheduling screens
**Date:** 2026-09-15 · **From:** Complaints design §6 R-CCM-11 · **Status:** DRAFT `[P2 for delivery]`
**Ask:** when an order line is applied in the order book (PPC-R-04 inbound apply, Excel import, manual creation) evaluate `v_qc_complaint_watch_board` for the line's customer and PSN; on a match write the watch-lot row (`mes_qc_complaint_watch_lot` with `order_line_id`) and raise QA_COMPLAINT_AFFECTED_ORDER with the corrective summary; the Order Book, Campaign Planning and Rolling Sequence screens show a COMPLAINT WATCH chip on matched lines with the summary and the PPC acknowledgement action (`ack_ppc_by / at`).
**Why:** BRD F3.3-04, 6.1.1 — PPC is told of a fresh order for a complaint-affected customer / PSN with the suggested corrective action before scheduling.
**Affects:** order inbound apply (PPC-R-04), Orders screen, Campaign Planning and Rolling Sequence screens (PPC-R-11).

### CCM-R-02 — Next-lot production events, the CAPA pulpit-note acknowledgement recorded on the watch lot, the clearance check
**Date:** 2026-09-15 · **From:** §6 R-CCM-12/13, §7 R-CCM-16 · **Status:** DRAFT `[P2 for delivery]`
**Ask:** on every confirmation whose material's customer (order line) and PSN match an ACTIVE watch, raise QA_COMPLAINT_NEXT_LOT and link the batch to the watch lot (`batch_id`, `heat_number`, `unit_operation_id`, `confirmation_id`, `produced_at`); the pulpit note of type CAPA generated by the Quality module (OPS-R-01, `source_ref` = complaint number, POPUP_ACK) records its acknowledgement on the watch lot (`ack_ops_by / at`) — the first confirmation of the lot at the unit needs it; the usage-decision auto-clearance (Quality) and the FG hand-over read the acknowledgements (`ack_ppc`, `ack_ops`, `ack_qa`) and refuse while one is missing for a lot under a pending effectiveness check.
**Why:** BRD F3.3-03, F3.4-02, 6.4 — the corrective action cannot be skipped at the next lot and the effectiveness check cannot be missed.
**Affects:** confirmation pipeline (event hook), pulpit notes (OPS-R-01), hand-over / clearance gates.
