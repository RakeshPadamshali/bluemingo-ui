Finishing lines — capability and plan load
Same basis as the Dashboard flow strip and the Master Gantt: every P* / D* order line is booked on a line that can run its supply condition and size. Among those, the plan fills a line before opening another — but only while that line can still finish the job by its delivery date, otherwise the order goes to a line that can. Each line then runs continuously: it takes the earliest-due job whose bar has arrived, and fills an idle window with a later job only when that job is out before the due job's bar lands. Idle hours shown per line are the mill not having delivered bar yet. PL-2 is the only line for PG* ground conditions, which is why it carries the most hours.
