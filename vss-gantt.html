Every order booked on its machines — Caster → Rolling → NDT → Bright Bar → Heat Treatment Window
One lane per machine; each bar is one order's booking (coloured per order, so an order is traceable across machines) — hover for order · customer · stage · times · MT. Orders are sequenced by delivery date (RDD week, descending size within the week — the rolling-cycle rule); the rolling mill runs at the sheet's size-wise budget productivity (968 / 605 / 550 MT/day, 90% yield) with Roll-Set changeovers (size 25 min · feeder 1.5 h · BDM 4 h); parallel lines (5 peeling + drawing, 6 furnaces) are filled least-loaded-first to balance load; orders covered by open billet stock skip the caster (short cover gets a top-up cast). Dashed lines mark day boundaries.
