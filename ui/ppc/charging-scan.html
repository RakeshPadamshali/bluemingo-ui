Charging Pool

What each released block allows into the charging pool — make-to-order blocks only the billets pegged to their order, make-to-stock blocks the cleared billets PPC chose — under the mill's charging-pool mode, with the charges seen so far. A read-only view: charging itself stays on the production-confirmation Charging screen.
Prototype on sample data — a read-only view; a reload resets. Try: switch the mill · click a block for its billets around the charge front (a held billet cannot be charged) · Refresh to read new charges · filter the billets · preview the block under another pool mode · Open Swapping for a lot the block needs.
Read-only. Charging happens on the production-confirmation application's Charging screen: with sequencing required before charge switched on for the mill, only the lots a release sequenced can be charged — enforced by that application. Where the installation enables scan to charge, the operator scans the label and the lot is selected from the list, or the screen says why it cannot be charged. A lot outside a release is swapped before the release (Swapping), never at the furnace.
