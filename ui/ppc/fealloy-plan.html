Ferro Alloy Requirement Plan

Monthly / campaign requirement from confirmed orders (editable selection) plus forecast months, computed from the alloy norms (grade / PSN / section), recovery and prices; stock from SMS against the plan; weekly plan-vs-actual variance per grade and heat.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: tick the 42CrMo4 forecast line (FeMn shortfall grows) · change 20MnCr5 to 250 t (230 t carries to November — see the November plan) · Auto-capture · Upload forecast / prices · switch the basis to confirmed only · Issue to SMS · filter the variance.

Plan lines required = planned t × norm ÷ recovery · a line may take part of an order; the rest carries to next month as a linked line
