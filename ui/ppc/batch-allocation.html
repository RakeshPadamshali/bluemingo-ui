SMS Batch Arrival — SMS → Mills

PPC's casting indent is loaded as the casting plan (planned heats with their orders) and sent to SMS MES; SMS MES reports the actual heats and their billets or blooms; PPC matches each actual heat to its planned heat and replays it through the existing services of the production-confirmation application — heat generation with the real heat number, charge (the automatic casting confirmation creates the cast lot), billet cutting creating the billets exactly as SMS cut them, each with its order. Quality SMS clearance and Mills follow as usual.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. SMS MES interface details pending — the arrival is built when the interface exists; this prototype simulates the reports. Until then SMS production reaches Mills through the Allocation pull, run by hand (run the pull now). Codes are resolved from master data.

Arrivals — per SMS heat, the replay steps planned ↔ actual heat · heat generation → charge (automatic casting confirmation) → billet cutting, through the existing services · a step done is never posted twice · click a heat for its steps and pieces

Discrepancies step: billet cutting — pieces checked against the order before the cut · hard (section, grade) stops the step · soft (length beyond ± 100 mm) may be overridden by PPC with a reason · or the piece is left free

Bought and customer-supplied billets / blooms on Quality acceptance the lot is created at the mill's charging stage; its order — the one on the PO line — comes through the Allocation application and the swap bridge

Free lots — attach by swap lots with no order · eligible orders pass the FREE_TO_SO swap rules (PSN, grade, section, input size blocking; length soft) · under inspection: may take an order, not released or charged until cleared · Attach opens Swapping pre-filled

Events every match, replay step, override and correction
