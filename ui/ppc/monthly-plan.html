Monthly Plan

One plan per mill and month: planned tonnage per value of the mill's campaign basis — product type or family for BRM, input size for BLM — and the periods it may not be rolled; versioned on every amendment. Campaigns are checked against it at finalisation.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: Amend → change RO 45–80 blocked dates to 25–26 Oct (CMP-BRM-2026-41 turns OK) or cut Bars · RO 20–40 below 6,420 t (warns) → switch to PPC-HOD → Approve · click v1 to see the older version · November → Create a draft or Upload · switch to BLM.
