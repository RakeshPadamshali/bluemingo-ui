Casting Indent — CI-2026-0187 → SMS (CCM2)

Balance-to-cast per grade / PSN / output size / day from four demand sources — campaign orders, as-cast sales, make-to-stock quantities and NCO filler; cast length from the Allocation application where it gives one, else the length master, within the caster limits; special requirements and stock; PPC priority hard lock; NCO and rejected heats re-open lines. Loaded into the production-confirmation application as the casting plan; sent to SMS MES once that interface exists.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: click a cast length (an alternate needs a reason; 13,500 mm is refused) · tick / untick a lock or Change priority · Add NCO filler · Declare NCO (1.8 t is inside the tolerance; 3.0 t is booked) · Load as casting plan · then a heat of line 1 is rejected · Override a special requirement · edit a line (line 3 to 150 t; line 2 with 25 heats is refused — 28.4 t per heat) · Add line (SO-91710/10; SO-90310/10 is on hold; SQ 250 is not cast) · delete a line · Download indent · Upload indent (6 rows: 1 new, 1 update, 1 delete, 3 invalid) — after the load, any change re-opens the casting plan.
SMS may schedule only priority-locked lines for the day; a priority change re-opens SMS planning. Billets per heat = heat weight (25 t) ÷ (cast length × section unit weight), rounded down; the last piece takes the remaining metal and its length is not tied to the length master.
