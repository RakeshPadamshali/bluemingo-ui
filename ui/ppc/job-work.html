Job Work — challan, "At vendor" hold, receipt, confirmation

A route stage done at an outside vendor is a job-work operation with one equipment row per vendor; the lot reaches it by its route or a next-operation correction. PPC writes and prints its own challan and holds the lots "At vendor" through the existing lot hold; each time the vendor returns material PPC records the delivery challan and quantities, lifts the hold and confirms production at the job-work operation for the slice returned — the sent lot in, the returned lot out with its new size or code, quantity, pieces and order, scrap and losses — and what is still at the vendor stays held.
Prototype on sample data — every control works; a reload resets. Try: on JWC-2026-0045 record the rest of R031020 (40 pcs, last receipt) · on JWC-2026-0044 record a part receipt — 60 of the 112 pieces — and see what stays at the vendor, held · New challan (Peeling, tick R031050 and R031144) · on JWC-2026-0046 record one lot with the Quality hold ticked, then let Quality clear it · try a receipt whose quantities exceed its slice · filter the challans · print a challan.
