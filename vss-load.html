Load per line — booked hours, tonnage, utilisation within its active span
Expand a line to see its order-wise allocation in sequence. Utilisation = booked hours ÷ the line's active span (first booking → last booking); a high figure means the line runs continuously, which is the goal here — green ≥ 85% (continuous), amber 60–85%, grey below. Parallel lines are filled least-loaded-first — remaining imbalance is capability-driven (e.g. only one peeling line runs PG* ground conditions). Rolling-mill hours use the sheet's size-wise budget productivity (968 MT/day · 605 for 23–29 mm · 550 for 58 mm, on input tonnage, 90% yield, 22 productive h/day) and Roll-Set changeovers (size 25 min · feeder 1.5 h · BDM 4 h), applied once per size run — orders are sequenced by RDD week, descending size within the week.
